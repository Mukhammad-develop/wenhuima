The Soft Banquet








我们选择桌游来体现和体验项目的原因，是都通过规则、时机和轮流机制运作，我们将诸如等待和优先级等看不见的系统转化为一种可游玩的体验，使玩家能够感受到规则以及系统的混合特性。

We chose board games to represent and experience the project because they all operate through rules, timing, and turn-based mechanisms. We transform invisible systems, such as waiting and prioritization, into a playable experience, allowing players to perceive the hybrid nature of rules and systems.












这些“问号卡”像系统中的波动，源于餐厅中不可预测的瞬间——失误、延迟与偏差。当卡片被触发，流程被打断，秩序开始偏移：有时获得机会，有时被迫后退。在不断的偏移与修正中，系统显露出真实状态——不是完美的机器，而是持续摇摆的关系网络。


These “question mark cards” are like fluctuations within a system, arising from unpredictable moments in a restaurant—mistakes, delays, and deviations. When a card is triggered, the flow is interrupted, and order begins to shift: sometimes opportunities arise, sometimes one is forced to step back. Amid continuous shifts and corrections, the system reveals its true state—not a perfect machine, but a constantly oscillating network of relationships.                 




                          
           





我们从菜品中提取出四种代表性形态，并转化为抽象符号：蓝色体块象征系统的柔性与适应；黄色结构体现秩序中的张力与冲突；绿色球体指向信息的积累与生长；粉色形态则表现个体在结构中的压缩与变形。四种元素共同呈现出系统在稳定与失衡之间不断生成与重组的动态状态。


We extract four representative forms from the dishes and transform them into abstract symbols: the blue mass symbolizes the system’s flexibility and adaptability; the yellow structure reflects tension and conflict within order; the green sphere points to the accumulation and growth of information; the pink form expresses the compression and deformation of individuals within the structure. Together, these four elements create a dynamic state in which the system oscillates continuously between stability and imbalance.
