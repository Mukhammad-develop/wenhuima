The Soft Banquet


本项目以思考为中心，探讨人类行为如何被算法优化与系统优化。通过隐藏对“错误”与“延迟”的占用，揭示在消费背后行为的系统逻辑。

This project centers on thinking about how algorithms and systems optimize human behavior. By concealing the roles of “errors” and “delays,” it reveals the systemic logic of behavior behind consumption.
