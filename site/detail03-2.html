Controlled Voices


















这组影像构建出一个象征性的权力场域：象征“民众”的小人被置于特权阶级的餐桌之上，成为被分配与被消耗的“食物”。中心的小人竭力守护手中的希望之火，尽管难以改变既定的命运，但那簇火焰始终微弱而持续地燃烧，成为不被熄灭的存在。
This set of imagery constructs a symbolic field of power: the small figure representing “the people” is placed upon the dining table of the privileged class, becoming “food” to be distributed and consumed. The central figure strives to protect the flame of hope in its hands;  Although it is difficult to change the predetermined fate, that flicker continues to burn faintly yet persistently, becoming an existence that cannot be extinguished.
