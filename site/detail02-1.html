Hidden Cartography: LGBTQI+




















本研究以四类空间为线索：支持机构、友好 club、社群聚集点与迁移路径。它们并非稳定的坐标，而是在经验与记忆中反复显现与消散的痕迹。
This study takes four types of spaces as its threads: support institutions, friendly clubs, community gathering points, and migration pathways. They are not fixed coordinates, but traces that repeatedly emerge and fade within experience and memory.













S 项目通过追寻这些痕迹的流动轨迹，尝试勾勒一张未被标注的地图——它不依附于可见的边界，而生成于关系、感知与共享之中。在这张地图里，不可见并非缺失，而是一种持续运作的存在，悄然影响着人如何进入城市、停留其间，并再次离开。

By tracing the flow of these traces, the project attempts to sketch an uncharted map—one that does not rely on visible boundaries, but instead emerges from relationships, perceptions, and shared experiences. Within this map, the invisible is not an absence, but a continuously operating presence, subtly influencing how people enter the city, dwell within it, and depart once again.
