STARRY



针对“恐惧黑症”群体的情感支持系统。项目内容从光影疗愈海报、数据反馈App到调整包装的全货架设计。通过多感官干预，将黑暗的恐惧转化为可感知的视觉体验。

An emotional support system for people with “fear of darkness.” The project spans from light-and-shadow therapeutic posters and a data-feedback app to full-shelf packaging design. Through multisensory intervention, it transforms the fear of darkness into a perceivable visual experience.
