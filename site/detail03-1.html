Controlled Voices













这组海报以碎片化拼贴呈现出喧哗的舆论场，情绪在叠加中被不断放大。表面的对立并非自发生成，而是由背后的阶级结构所引导——它分配可见性与话语权，决定谁被看见、谁被淹没。网络暴力因此不只是情绪的爆发，而是阶级在信息流中的一种隐形操控。



This series of posters presents a noisy field of public opinion through fragmented collage, where emotions are continuously amplified through layering. The apparent opposition on the surface is not spontaneously generated but is guided by power, determining who is seen and who is submerged. Cyberbullying, therefore, is not merely an outburst of emotion, but a form of invisible manipulation of class within the flow of information.






















 这组海报通过展览构建出一个被信息与情绪包围的舆论空间。观者在其中游走，被不断放大的标题与图像所牵引。展览不指向单一事件，而是揭示背后的结构——海报对于观者的无形影响，也是阶级对于舆论无形影响的具像化：调配注意力、放大冲突，并悄然塑造判断。



 This series of posters constructs, through the exhibition, a discursive space surrounded by information and emotion. Viewers move within it, drawn by continuously amplified headlines and images. The exhibition does not point to a single event. Instead, it reveals the underlying structure—the invisible influence of posters on viewers, and the materialization of the invisible influence of class on public opinion: orchestrating attention, amplifying conflict, and subtly shaping judgment.
