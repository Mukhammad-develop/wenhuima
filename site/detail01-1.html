The Soft Banquet







本组菜品提取形态与质感，转化为流动、扩张、积累与压缩四种状态，对应系统中的适应、张力、生长与约束。菜品不再只是食物，而成为系统运行的节点，在感知与语言中构建持续生成的关系网络。


This set of dishes extracts form and texture, transforming them into four states: flow, expansion, accumulation, and compression, corresponding to adaptation, tension, growth, and constraint within a system. The dishes are no longer merely food, but become nodes in the system’s operation, constructing a continuously evolving network of relationships through perception and language.

















 这组诗歌并非对菜品的描述，而是对其内在状态的转译。每一道菜被视为一个感知节点，通过视觉、触感与时间的变化，被重新组织为一种系统语言。诗歌以身体与感官为入口，将流动、积累与生成的过程转化为可感知的叙事，使菜品从具体对象转向关系与机制的表达。
This set of poems is not a description of dishes, but a translation of their internal states. Each dish is regarded as a perceptual node, reorganized into a systemic language through visual, tactile, and temporal transformations. The poems take the body and senses as their entry point, transforming processes of flow, accumulation, and generation into perceptible narratives, allowing the dishes to shift from concrete objects toward expressions of relationships and mechanisms.
