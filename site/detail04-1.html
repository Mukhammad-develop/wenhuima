STARRY







项目构建了一套围绕睡眠与情绪调节的交互系统：通过首页的个性化内容推送、社区中的经验共享、冥想与自我练习模块、音频助眠选择，以及睡眠记录与数据可视化分析，用户得以在使用过程中不断感知、调整并理解自身状态。系统将日常行为转化为持续反馈的过程，使情绪支持与自我调节在无形中发生。

The project has built an interactive system centered on sleep and emotional regulation: through personalized content recommendations on the homepage, experience sharing within the community, meditation and self-practice modules, audio options for sleep assistance, as well as sleep tracking and data visualization analysis, users can continuously perceive, adjust, and understand their own state during use. The system transforms daily behaviors into an ongoing feedback process, allowing emotional support and self-regulation to occur seamlessly and naturally.











该项目以“暗”为品牌核心，通过统一的包装设计语言整合睡眠眼罩与香薰产品。以低饱和色彩与克制排版传达静谧与安全感，使包装不仅是产品外壳，更成为连接用户情绪与品牌体验的关键媒介。
This project makes “darkness” the core of its brand, integrating sleep masks and aromatherapy products under a unified packaging design language. It uses low-saturation colors and restrained typography to convey a sense of tranquility and security, making the packaging not just a product shell but a key medium that connects users’ emotions to the brand experience.
