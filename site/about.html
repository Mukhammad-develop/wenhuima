我关注那些不可见却持续运作的系统。
在我的设计中，技术、规则与人并不是彼此分离的对象，而是不断交织、相互作用的关系网络。
我的项目通常以日常情境为切入点，通过对行为、结构与机制的分析，揭示隐藏在表象之下的运行逻辑。我通过材料、空间与叙事，将这些抽象的系统转化为可被感知的体验。
对我而言，设计不仅是表达与产出，更是一种理解社会与世界的方式。


I focus on systems that are invisible yet continuously operating. In my design, technology, rules, and people are not separate entities, but rather an interwoven network of relationships that constantly interact.
My projects usually take everyday situations as entry points, and through the analysis of behavior, structure, and mechanisms, reveal the underlying logic hidden beneath surface appearances. Through materials, space, and narrative, I transform these abstract systems into experiences that can be perceived.
For me, design is not only about expression and production, but also a way of understanding society and the world.
