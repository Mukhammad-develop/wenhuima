Controlled Voices



以网络暴力为意识形态，剖析其背后的阶层冲突与社会结构。通过海报与影像，探讨个体在权力系统中的劳动力，并试图在对抗与重构中寻找希望的可能。

Taking cyberbullying as an ideology, it analyzes the class conflicts and social structures behind it. Through posters and imagery, it explores the individual as labor within systems of power and seeks the possibility of hope through resistance and reconstruction.
